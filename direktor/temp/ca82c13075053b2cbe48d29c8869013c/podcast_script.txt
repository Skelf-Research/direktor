This cuts out the need for complex manual coding, making the entire process more streamlined and accessible.

This rapid retooling means they can quickly adapt to new models or customizations. Additionally, the democratization of skills has been a game-changer. Small and medium-sized enterprises have seen a 50% increase in the adoption of robotic systems, thanks to the fact that RoboGPT lowers the barrier to entry for non-technical staff.

This enhancement not only improves precision but also helps reduce fatigue during long procedures. On the rehabilitation front, physical therapists are using RoboGPT to easily customize rehabilitation robots for individual patient needs. This has led to a 40% improvement in patient outcomes.
